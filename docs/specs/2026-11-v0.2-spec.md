## 0. What we learned

### 0.1 Scan Feed (scanfeed.co, studied signed in)

| Part | What it does | Lesson for Reely |
|---|---|---|
| **Extension "Sort"** | On any IG / TikTok / FB profile, sorts the whole feed by outlier score (vs that channel's normal; IG uses likes, TikTok views). 25 posts ≈ 15 s. Runs in the user's own logged-in Chrome, so Instagram data is complete and free for them. | Their "15 seconds" is **one channel**, not a search. Channel-first research is fast and precise because the user already picked a good channel. |
| **Discovery** | Type a topic → AI returns *channels*, in two groups: "direct" and "adjacent audience — borrow the format", each with a reason line. 👎 hides a channel for good. Also suggests search words for TikTok and IG. | Recommending **channels to study** answers "which channel?" before "which clip?". |
| **Shared library** | Every public clip at 3×+ found by any user's Sort joins the Discovery library anonymously (opt-out in privacy settings). | A data flywheel. Reely already stores every fetched video and creator but never searches them. |
| **Capture / Brief** | Save button on IG / TikTok, paste a link, tags, transcripts, AI Hook/Body/CTA; script templates and a task board. | Saving from anywhere is the habit loop. Brief is covered by Reely's scripts and 7-day plan. |
| **Pricing** | Free: 25 latest posts, 20 saves. Pro ฿1,990/yr. | Thai creators and SMEs pay for this category. |

**Their weak spot:** they never ask what the product is. Discovery reduced "ai บัญชี" to "ai business tools" and returned
general AI and marketing channels.

### 0.2 Subscribr (from its help docs; the competitors page needs sign-in)

- **Competitors:** 5–10 channels "similar in niche and size". Shows stats, a **Recent Outliers** feed (threshold
  1.5× / 2× / 3×, Shorts filter) and a weekly email.
- **Intel:** 100K+ channels. Outlier score = views ÷ the average of ~10 neighbouring videos. Velocity score for fast-growing
  small channels. Similar channels and "chat with a channel".
- **Channel audit** of your own channel, and **MCP** so AI agents can use the data.

### 0.3 Reely's own data (last 14 days, 32 researches)

| Measure | Instagram | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Kept after filters | **107 of 1,003 (11%)** | ~25% |
| Dropped: under the view floor | **626 (62%)** | ~55% |
| Dropped: older than the window | 152 (15%) | ~1% |
| Dropped: off-topic | 87 (9%) | ~20% |

- Run time: **35–87 s**. Only 5 👍/👎 votes in 14 days. One `/api/plan` timeout (60 s) on 2026-10-27.
- **Instagram root cause:** keyword search returns ~12 reels per keyword (popular search, English only), mostly small
  accounts, and Thai can't be searched at all. More keyword calls won't fix it. Instagram has to be found **through
  creators**: find the right accounts, then mine their feeds.

## 1. The promise

> **Reely helps founders find the right creators, see what actually works for them, and turn it into a marketing plan
> for their own product.**

The central question every feature answers: *can Reely consistently find the right creators, and explain which of their
content strategies a founder can adapt?* The unit of value is **relevant marketing opportunities**, not the number of videos.

Reely is not another Scan Feed (videos inside one account) or Subscribr (competitor monitoring). It combines both with
what neither does: understanding the user's product and judging which strategies transfer to it.

## 3. Work by phase (one session per item)

### Phase 1 — Foundation: reliability and search quality

**1.1 UX fixes** (0 credits) — ✅ shipped 2026-11-01
- **Back from a clip.** Today `app/[locale]/app/video/[id]/page.tsx:164` links to `/app` (the new-research chat). Cards link
  with `?from=<research id>` (also from Saved, Plan and Tracking). Back returns there ("กลับไปผลลัพธ์"), restoring filter,
  sort and scroll from sessionStorage. With no `from`: `router.back()` when there's history, else `/app`.
- **Product editing.** Edit opens a right-side panel on desktop and a full-screen sheet on mobile. Sections: Basics (with
  fill-from-link) · Audience & goal · Competitors & Inspiration · Words to avoid. Sticky Cancel / Save, an unsaved-changes
  warning, and the card stays in place.
- **Ask Reely.** One input with a 📎 paste-a-clip button (preview chip with cover and @handle) and 3 prompts picked for this
  research that **send on tap**. After an answer or at the end of results, at most 4 next steps that fit the context
  (write a script · more like this, then choose same format / worldwide / product accounts · follow · edit the request).
  A floating "ถาม Reely" button appears only while the input is off-screen.
- Done when: research → clip → back lands on the same results and scroll; every chip acts on one tap; editing a product
  never moves other cards.

**1.2 Reliable sending** (0 credits) — ✅ built 2026-11-01, plus Ask Reely answer cards (clips, hooks, saved scripts), a stored thread and a docked composer
- Each send gets a request ID, carried to the server and into any error log. The prompt text is not logged.
- A retry reuses the same ID, so it can't start a second research.
- Loading and disabled states always say why; a failed send keeps the text in the box.
- The plan request shows "taking longer than usual — retry" after 30 s.
- Events go to a new `client_events` table (`send_attempt`, `send_failed`, `plan_timeout`, `save`, `vote`, with a source).
  Vercel Analytics custom events need the Pro plan, so we keep our own.
- Still open: which box, device and browser your friend used.

**1.3 Relevance test set and metrics** (0 credits; stored results) — ✅ built 2026-11-01 (owner page /app/eval)
- **12 core briefs** from real use (AccOS Pro, Aetox, the fitness app, AirLearn, FNKvision, GoChek, the skincare brand,
  the microphone product, …), mixing Thai, English and mixed briefs, and SaaS, apps and e-commerce.
- Labels 2 / 1 / 0 (on target / same audience / off) from an AI judge with a strict rubric, your 👍/👎, and a small
  labelling page for corrections.
- The set grows to **30+** by adding real researches. Replaying stored results is free; live re-runs stay on the 12 core
  briefs (~40 credits each).
- Metrics, per platform and Thai vs English:

  | Metric | Measures | v0.2 target |
  |---|---|---|
  | Creator precision@5 | Are recommended creators relevant? | ≥ 0.8 |
  | Clip precision@10 | Are returned clips useful? | TikTok ≥ 0.8, IG ≥ 0.7 |
  | Coverage | Relevant IG clips per IG run | ≥ 15 |
  | Candidates found vs ranked | Relevant items found at all, apart from how they're ranked | tracked |
  | Time to first useful result | First on-target clip on screen | ≤ 15 s |
  | Cost per useful clip | Credits ÷ clips labelled 2 | tracked, falls over time |
  | Diversity | Share of top 20 from the 3 biggest creators | ≤ 40% |

- `npm run eval:search` prints the table. The test set is not called proof of "best in the world"; it is the regression
  check every search change must pass.

**1.4 Creator relevance** (0 credits) — ✅ shipped 2026-11-01. Measured on 6 re-runs with the steady judge (v3): TikTok P@10 58→64%, YouTube 58→70%, IG 51→47%, creators@5 41→38%, concentration 27→38%. Modest gain on TikTok/YouTube; creators@5 did not move, so creator discovery (1.5) is the next lever
- Judge the **account** from its feed captions (already fetched), not just one clip. Off-topic accounts are dropped with
  their clips.
- The same AI call returns `accountType` (product/brand · creator · media · founder), which feeds the Phase 3 filter.
- Cards show a one-line "why it matches".

**1.5 Instagram creator-first** — ✅ shipped 2026-11-01 (with 1.6 known creators as seeds). Measured on 6 re-runs (139 credits): IG useful Reels 2.0→5.2 per research, credits per useful clip 1.9, run time 58→49 s, TikTok P@10 57→65%, YouTube 64→73%; IG P@10 flat at 50%, creators@5 flat (36→35%). Targets (IG useful ≥15, IG P@10 ≥70%) not reached yet. Tuning tried 2026-11-01: a mixed first step (one account per source) was not better (IG P@10 52% vs 50%, 25 vs 37 feed Reels, 2.8 vs 1.9 credits per useful clip), so priority order stays and the other sources are a fallback when the first step is weak. Fallback measured on the same 6 briefs (114 credits): IG useful 5.5 per research, 45 feed Reels (vs 37 and 25), credits per useful clip 1.7 (vs 1.9 and 2.8), 48 s; IG P@10 44% (vs 50% and 52%). The P@10 dip is within run-to-run noise (TikTok, which none of these changes touch, moved 65→60%; YouTube 73→76%), so the fallback stays for more Reels at fewer credits. SaaS AI สำหรับนักบัญชี found 0 on-topic Reels in all three versions: there are few Thai accounting Reels accounts to seed from, which step logic can't fix. Further step tuning stopped here (≤ 25 credits per run, spent in steps)
- **Seeds** in order: the user's known creators (1.6) · the product's Competitors / Inspiration lists · Instagram account
  search with up to 3 keywords (Thai *and* English) → `pickRelevantAccounts` · AI-suggested well-known accounts (checked
  with one feed page; a missing profile is free) · the run's top TikTok / YouTube creators under the same handle.
- **Step 1:** 3–5 best seeds × 2 pages. **Step 2**, only if step 1 found relevant clips: up to 10 accounts, still within
  25 credits.
- **Scoring:** usual views = median of the creator's recent reels without the scored one (as now), needing **≥ 8 reels**
  before scoring. Keep a reel at ≥ 2× usual with no absolute floor. Absolute views stay an optional user filter and a
  ranking factor.
- The results header says what the search did: "ดู 6 บัญชี เจอ 14 Reels ที่ตรงสินค้า".
- Done when: on the test set, IG keeps ≥ 15 relevant clips per run at IG clip precision@10 ≥ 0.7.

**1.6 Known creators (private)** (0 credits)
- Before searching, look up creators that *this user's* earlier researches rated relevant for the same product or category.
  They become seeds and appear first, already scored from the 24 h cache.

**1.7 Results as they arrive** (0 credits)
- Results stream per platform and per stage (known creators → TikTok → YouTube → Instagram mining). Target: first useful
  results ≤ 15 s; the full run may take longer.

### Phase 2 — The creator experience

**2.1 Channels to Study** on every research: **ตรงสาย** (direct) and **คนดูกลุ่มเดียวกัน — ยืมฟอร์แมตได้** (same audience,
borrow the format), each with a reason ("reaches the same audience as your product and often demos software in short
tutorials") and hit count. Hide with undo. No extra credits.

**2.2 Channel page** `/app/channel/[platform]/[handle]`:
- Profile and linked platforms, usual vs best views, posting rhythm.
- Feed sorted by outlier score; also views, likes, comments, newest; 30 d, 90 d or 1 y.
- **Strategy breakdown** (one AI call): content pillars, recurring hooks, formats and length, what the top hits share, and
  **what transfers to your product**.
- Save, track, export, and write a script from a clip.
- Cost on open: TikTok 2, IG 2–3, YouTube free; 24 h cache.

**2.3 Competitors / Inspiration lists per product** (up to 10 channels): one outlier feed refreshed on open at most once per
24 h, with threshold 1.5× / 2× / 3× and a platform filter. Tracked accounts move into these lists.
- Tests your hypothesis: save and 👍 rates for clips from listed channels vs keyword clips (from `client_events`).

### Phase 3 — Intelligence layer

**3.1 Shared library**, built in three layers from day one:

| Layer | Holds | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Private research | Briefs, searches, notes, feedback, lists | The user only |
| Shared creators | Public handle, platform, topic tags, usual views, `accountType` | Everyone |
| Shared clips | Public link, stats, outlier score, fetched-at, source | Everyone |

Safeguards:
- Nothing from the private layer ever reaches the shared layers, including briefs, competitor lists and verdicts.
- A creator enters the shared layer only after **2+ different users'** researches rated it relevant, so the library can't
  show what one person researched.
- Each row keeps its source link, fetch time and provider. Stats older than 7 days refresh only when shown in a top 10.
  Clips unseen for 180 days are deleted.
- A contribution switch on the account page, on by default. Final retention rules are checked against the providers' terms
  before launch.
- Search uses caption embeddings (pgvector) plus topic tags. Library results show first as "จากคลัง Reely".

**3.2 Account-type filter:** All · Product/Brand · Creators · Media, using `accountType` from 1.4. Official product accounts
are told apart from reviewers; founders' own accounts count. It becomes a tab if usage shows people rely on it.

**3.3 Save to Reely extension** (Chrome / Edge MV3):
- One button on IG / TikTok / YouTube post pages and profiles, plus a right-click menu. Only the URL is sent; it is read
  with the existing import (1 credit, cached) into a chosen product and folder.
- Profiles get "Open in Reely" (the channel page).
- Permissions are limited to those three sites plus Reely. No cookies, no browsing history, sign-in through a short-lived
  token from the Reely account page. No automated collection.

**3.4 Better Thai account discovery:** library seeds plus learned Thai search words per category.

### Phase 4 — Later

- **My Channels, public audit:** paste your own links → your hits vs your normal, best formats and topics, gaps vs your
  Competitors / Inspiration lists, 5 next ideas. Labelled as public numbers, not private analytics.
- **Account login (OAuth):** Instagram API with Instagram Login (`instagram_business_manage_insights`, Meta App Review);

  accounts). Only after clear demand.
- **MCP server:** read-only `search_creators`, `get_creator_outliers`, `get_research_report`, built on the Phase 1–3
  service layer.

## 4. Success for v0.2

1. Describing a product returns **relevant creators**, not generic viral accounts (creator precision@5 ≥ 0.8).
2. Instagram returns a meaningful number of relevant clips (≥ 15 per IG run) instead of discarding most.
3. Users save creators and come back to their latest outliers without repeating a research.
4. Measurable relevance gains on the test set, in Thai and English.
5. From any clip, a user understands how to adapt its approach to their product.
6. **Share of research sessions where the user saves at least one creator or clip.** This is the main sign of a
   recurring workspace.
