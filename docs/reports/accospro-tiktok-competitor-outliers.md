# Competitor Outlier Report: FlowAccount vs PEAK on TikTok (for AccOs Pro)

Prepared 2026-10-25 with the `outlier-post-finder` and `competitor-social-research` skills.
Data: ScrapeCreators, public posts only. Credits used: 7 (5 data + 2 transcripts).

## Executive summary

- **Biggest opportunity:** the posts that break out are **business-owner lessons and practical
  checklists, not product content**. Neither competitor's top post mentions its software.
- **Competitor to study closest:** **PEAK**. With a similar audience (20.6K vs 22.8K followers), its
  normal post gets **~7× more views** than FlowAccount's (median 3,639 vs 499).
- **Format to test first:** a 45–60 s talking-head "business lesson" with a fear or curiosity hook,
  tied to a known Thai brand or a real statistic.

## Repeatable patterns

1. **The lesson, not the product.** All four huge outliers teach owners something (why businesses
   fail, how a brand grew, SME survival odds). The software is never mentioned. Product updates sit
   around 1× (e.g. FlowAccount's mobile-app update).
2. **Fear or survival hooks.** "เจ๊ง" (going bust), "ครึ่งเดียว" (only half survive),
   "คุมเข้ม" (tightening checks). Owners stop for threats to their business.
3. **Borrowed authority.** An expert at an expo, Bank of Thailand data, a famous Thai brand.
4. **Save-worthy utility.** Checklists and deadlines earn saves and shares out of proportion to views
   (FlowAccount's checklist: 132 saves on 6.5K views).
5. **The same clip can win or flop.** FlowAccount posted the "why businesses fail" clip twice on
   the same day: one got 41,320 views, the other 1,405. Re-posting a strong clip with a new hook or
   cover is a cheap test.
6. **PEAK's consistency.** PEAK's ordinary posts beat FlowAccount's ordinary posts by ~7×, using the
   same audience hashtag on almost every post (#เจ้าของธุรกิจ, "business owner").

- "ผู้ประกอบการเตรียมรับมือไว้ได้เลยครับ" — PEAK, 4.9×

Adapt the structure, not the words or footage.

## Recommended content tests (next 2 weeks)

1. **Business-lesson clip** (talking head, 45–60 s): a fear hook about why Thai SMEs run into tax or
   cash trouble, one concrete lesson, ending with a question. Success: ≥ 5× AccOs Pro's usual views.
2. **Checklist for accounting offices** (video or carousel): "5 things to prepare before month-end
   close." Success: saves ≥ 2% of views.
3. **Re-post test:** re-post AccOs Pro's best existing clip with a new first line and cover a week
   later. Success: either version reaches ≥ 3× usual views.

- Views measure attention, not sign-ups or sales.
- Reasons for success are inferred from captions and transcripts.
